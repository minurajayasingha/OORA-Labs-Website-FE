A Colombo-Based Software Team

OORA Labs is a Colombo-based software team founded in 2023. We design and build custom applications, business tools, and AI-driven solutions for companies that need technology to work the way they do. We also build our own products — CRM, supply chain, and micro-finance platforms among them — which keeps our engineering sharp and our advice practical.
